# Storylines

Three different films about one thing: Ambient makes the phone call you do not want to make, and it cannot be talked into a bad deal. Each is 30 to 40 s at 1920x1080, 30 fps. Every voice line, number and screen comes from the demo call in `docs/demo/smoke-transcript.txt` and the real UI captured in `assets/ui/`.

## A. "Plus parts." (recommended)

- **Device:** the feature as the hook, told in medias res. **Form:** the call rebuilds in one frame, with match cuts on one shape, the ring (the Ambient mark, the incoming-call rings, the Echo's light ring, the booked check).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the film opens inside the call, at the moment a person would have been cheated. A plumber's voice says "Yes, plus parts." and a calm voice answers "Sorry. I need one total price." The viewer leans in, the film rewinds to show how the call got here, then returns to the moment and lets it resolve: relief, then trust.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 already holds the plumber's line in huge type, "plus parts" in the accent, with a phone-line waveform under it. The voice lands at 0.2 s. At 1.4 s the reply arrives in Ambient's voice and blue.
- **Beats:** hook (the trap) → rewind → "Alexa, handle the call." → the disclosure, first words → over the limit, the Show asks the owner → the owner offers, the plumber counters → back to the trap, now understood → refusal, read-back, clean yes → booked, with the proof → breadth in four quick cuts → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the real product UI, large, on a warm graphite ground; one cobalt accent for Ambient's words; the booked green only on the payoff.
- **Sound:** the call itself, in two voices on a phone band, carries the film; the bed stays near silent until the payoff; effects only on visible actions.

## B. "The call you keep not making"

- **Device:** a moment in someone's day, then life before and after. **Form:** object-to-object chain.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a phone shows "Joe's Plumbing" for the third evening running; the thumb hovers and backs away while a tap drips. The dread is familiar. One sentence to Alexa hands the call off, and the drip stops at the end.
- **First two seconds:** a phone screen, 7:42 pm, the contact "Joe's Plumbing" and a call button; the drip sound at 0.3 s.
- **Beats:** dread → hand-off → the call as type → owner decision on the Show → booked → the drip stops → end card.
- **Look:** a dark evening phone screen against warm light; product UI after the hand-off.
- **Sound:** the drip as the clock; it stops on the booking.

## C. "You're the plumber"

- **Device:** a question with the input, in the second person. **Form:** split screen to one: the business's phone on the left, the owner's Echo Show on the right, collapsing to one frame at the yes.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the viewer is put in the plumber's shoes. The phone rings, and the caller says first that it is an AI. The viewer tries the oldest trick, "plus parts", and loses. It is funny and fair to both sides.
- **First two seconds:** "Your phone rings. It's an AI." over the real incoming-call screen with the "AI assistant" badge.
- **Beats:** the ring → the disclosure → the quote → the owner's side lights up → the trick → refused → the business gets a receipt → end card with "Try to fool it yourself."
- **Look:** two real UIs side by side, then one.
- **Sound:** the ring, the two voices, the receipt chime.
