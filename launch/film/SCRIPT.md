# Script: "Plus parts." (36.5 s launch film)

Every spoken line is from the demo call in `docs/demo/smoke-transcript.txt`, shortened. Every screen is the real product, captured from `/sim` by `launch/capture/capture.py`. The Alexa+ host is simulated, and a label says so whenever the Echo Show is on screen. The exact sound times live in `tools/mix.py`; the picture reads them from `cues.js`.

Footage: Pexels clips (free license), cut and graded by `tools/footage.sh`. Faces are cropped out, so no person appears to endorse the product.

Voices: Joe (Joe's Plumbing) and Ambient speak on a phone band. Maya speaks in the room. All three voices are Kokoro text to speech. The score is original, generated locally with MusicGen.

## Why it is built this way

- The hook is the most surprising true moment of the product: a business tries an old trick, and the AI refuses it. A judge sees an AI on a real call in the first frame and hears the trick at 0.15 s.
- The price is the one object of the film. It rolls $180, $150, $160, and the trap and its refusal happen on it. The research on 22 launch films found that the strongest films turn the idea into one object.
- Maya is the person with a stake: her budget and a stranger on the phone. Story and emotion was the weakest score in 21 of the 22 reference films.
- The film returns to its opening frame for the payoff, as the Opus 5.5 film returns to its opening horizon.
- Blind critic rounds against the Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Poke films shaped it. Round 1 asked for a world, so the call now runs over footage. Round 2 asked for brighter plates, one spoken line at a time, a longer payoff and a real end hit.
- The end card gives one next action: the phrase to say.
